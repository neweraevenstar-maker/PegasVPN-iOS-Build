## Privacy Policy — working draft

PegasVPN routes selected device network traffic through a VPN server when connected. The service processes account authentication information (Apple identity or email where provided), device installation identifiers, VPN public keys and assigned tunnel configuration, subscription verification information and technical data required for security and operation. Apple processes App Store payments; PegasVPN verifies signed purchase information to determine service access.

**Before publication, operator must supply:** actual server/Nginx/system logs and IP retention, backup retention, email/OTP and account record retention, restoration record retention, third-party processor roles and destinations, data subject contact and deletion exceptions, cross-border transfer details. These facts must not be fabricated. No 'no logs' claim is authorized.

Account deletion is available in-app. Deletion revokes VPN access but does not cancel an Apple subscription; customers cancel separately in Apple Account subscriptions. Some records may be retained when legally required or for narrowly defined security/billing purposes, with actual categories and retention periods to be specified before publication.

## Terms — working draft

PegasVPN provides a subscription VPN service subject to service availability and applicable law. Monthly and yearly subscription products are offered through Apple's in-app purchase system. Prices shown at checkout are localized StoreKit prices. An introductory free trial of seven days may be available to eligible customers; unless cancelled, the subscription automatically renews at the displayed price and period. Manage or cancel in Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting a PegasVPN account does not cancel the Apple subscription.

Service locations are limited to configured operational servers. Do not promise specific speed, uninterrupted availability, anonymity, bypass of restrictions or access in prohibited jurisdictions.

**Before publication, legal review must fill:** full company contact and address, governing law and consumer protections, acceptable use, refund wording consistent with Apple policy, termination, complaints, limitations subject to applicable law, effective date.

## App Store review text — prepared

The app uses NetworkExtension Packet Tunnel to establish a WireGuard VPN connection to an operator-controlled server. Sign-in options include Apple and email OTP; guest access is supported. A verified account is required before purchase. In-app subscription products are monthly and yearly, with a seven-day introductory trial only where StoreKit confirms eligibility. The app offers Restore Purchases and in-app account deletion. The app discloses VPN traffic routing and account/connection data use before VPN use or purchase.
