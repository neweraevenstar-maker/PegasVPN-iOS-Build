PegasVPN Privacy Policy

Operator: VIM VITAE LIMITED, Hong Kong. Contact: info@vimvitae.net.

Draft — not approved for publication. Production logging, retention, backups, and third-party processing have not yet been independently verified.

Information processed

The app and backend process account identifiers, optional email or Apple sign-in identity, session credentials, random installation identifiers, WireGuard public keys and assigned tunnel IP addresses, subscription transactions and entitlement status, and technical service requests. Apple handles payments. Email sign-in codes are sent through an email delivery provider.

VPN traffic

When connected, selected network traffic is routed through a VPN endpoint. The provider may technically observe connection metadata. The actual logging of network metadata, IP addresses and traffic must be audited before final publication. No no-logs or anonymity promise is made.

Purposes and recipients

Data is used to authenticate accounts, provision VPN access, validate subscriptions, protect against abuse, maintain security, respond to support requests and manage deletion. Apple, the hosting provider and email delivery provider perform necessary service functions. Their identities, locations, processing arrangements and retention must be confirmed. VPN traffic data is not intended to be sold or used for advertising; operational practices must be verified against Apple's VPN policy.

Retention

Source code includes expiry-based cleanup for OTP codes, sessions, refresh credentials and rate-limiting records. Seven days for application logs, 180 days for Apple notifications and 30 days after deletion plus expiry for paid-account recovery are proposed settings, not verified production retention. Server and system logs, database backups and third-party provider logs have not been audited. Exact category-specific retention will be published before launch.

Account deletion and rights

Delete an account in the app via Account → Delete account. This revokes VPN access and deletes live account records. A limited purchase-recovery mapping may be retained for a still-active paid entitlement, pending a finalized retention policy. Backups and provider records have separate deletion schedules. Account deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions. For access, correction or deletion requests contact info@vimvitae.net.

Security and changes

VPN transport uses WireGuard. New client private keys are held on-device; legacy records and backups require separate review. This notice will receive an effective date once approved and updated as practices change.
